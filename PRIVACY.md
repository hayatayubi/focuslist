# FocusList privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

FocusList is a to-do list app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It does not collect any data.

- **No account.** You don't sign up or sign in.
- **No tracking, analytics or ads.** The app contains no third-party code that tracks you.
- **Your tasks stay with you.** Tasks are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also synced between your own devices through your private iCloud storage. The developer can't see them.
- **Reminders** are scheduled on your device using the system's notifications. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **Widgets** read your tasks from the app's storage on the same device.

If you delete the app, its data on that device is deleted. Data in your iCloud can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/hayatayubi/focuslist/issues.
