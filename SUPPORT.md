# FocusList support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue here:
https://github.com/hayatayubi/focuslist/issues

Some quick answers:

- **Tasks not syncing between iPhone and Mac?** Make sure both are signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud Drive turned on. Open the app on each device to sync.
- **Reminders not showing?** Check Settings › Notifications › FocusList.
- **Ticking a task off in the widget:** tap the circle next to the task.
- **Swipe actions (iPhone):** swipe right to complete, left to reschedule or delete.
- **Trackpad swipes (Mac):** two-finger swipe right to complete, left to delete. ⌘Z undoes.
